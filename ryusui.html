About | Ryusui Nanami

Ryusui Nanami is a character from the anime Dr. Stone. He is a confident, ambitious, and wealthy young man who loves money, adventure, and sailing. Ryusui is an excellent sailor and a skilled negotiator who is always eager to explore the world and achieve his goals. Despite his greedy nature, he is also brave, intelligent, and willing to help his friends rebuild civilization.
